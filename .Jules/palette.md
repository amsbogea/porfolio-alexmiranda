## 2026-10-24 - Focus Indicators on Dark Themes
**Learning:** High-contrast dark executive UI themes require explicit `:focus-visible` outline styles with appropriate `outline-offset` and `border-radius` matching pill/rounded buttons to ensure WCAG 2.4.7 compliance without visual distortion.
**Action:** Always apply `:focus-visible` with matching `border-radius` for pill-shaped CTA links and buttons using high-contrast design tokens.
